## Scope

Sixth proposal for caritas-africa.org at `/caritas-v6/` (user request, 2026-10-19, after reviewing v5): a version "much less inspired by caritas.org". Home page first (index.html, Persuade); the seven inner pages follow once the user validates the direction. Until then, links to those pages open a notice with a link to the v2 page instead of a 404.

## Audience and job

Partners and funders (credibility, reports, governance), the 46 national Caritas (resources, network), individual donors (give now), candidates and providers (jobs, tenders). "Signaler un abus" stays one click away. Proof: Caritas Africa's own photographs (credited), mission, vision, publications with PDFs, dated statements and tenders, APPROCHE programme figures. No invented figures; nothing copied from caritas.org or the reference sites.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The NGO standard at the craft of CRS, CAFOD/Trócaire, ICRC and MSF, dressed in Caritas Africa's own print identity from its Strategic Framework 2024–2030: the home page opens like that cover. It refuses v5's caritas.org rounded panels, v2's condensed poster and v1's ruled ledger.

OWN-WORLD: White ground and full-bleed fields with straight edges: coral #F96874, coral wash #FFF0F0, deep wine #3D0507; logo wine #7C0800 for headings, links and actions. Poppins 700/600 sentence-case headings, Poppins italic for scripture and taglines, Source Sans 3 for text. Photos, buttons and cards as rounded squares (user's choice, 2026-10-19: no hexagonal or cut-corner shapes); coral rounded-square bullets and cross badge; the cover's coral wave with a wine edge under the hero and over the footer.

STORY: The visitor reads the motto, sees Caritas Africa's people and can give. Then: who we are in facts, four domains of action, mission and vision in scripture, the APPROCHE programme, the six zones, news and tenders, reports, an appeal, safeguarding, partners.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wine utility bar (Signaler un abus, Emplois, Contact, flags FR/EN/PT). White header: logo, five sentence-case links with dropdowns, rounded wine "Faire un don". Hero as the cover: H1 in wine Poppins top-left on three to four balanced lines, italic tagline, lead, two rounded buttons; right, two rounded-square frames overlapping on a diagonal (the large one carries the three-photo carousel, the small one a portrait), a coral rounded square and a rounded outline behind; the coral wave closes the viewport with the photo credit and carousel controls.

SIGNATURE: On load the frames open (clip-path inset with rounded corners grows from a small rounded square to the full frame, staggered, the coral square and cross badge settling last), then the large frame crossfades every 7s. Motion grammar: ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1); presses scale 0.97; photo hover 1.03 on fine pointers only; reduced motion shows frames at rest and stops autoplay.

FORM: canon, the standing exit of direction round 8c3b8f25 (user choice "Standard ONG, propre"); references CRS, CAFOD / Trócaire, ICRC, MSF; seed key 8c3b8f25.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
