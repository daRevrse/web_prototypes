# Surface: caritas-v8 — menu et guide de style

Mode: Read (a style guide the client reads to approve the global design), framed by the live site header and footer.
Audience: the Caritas Africa manager, NM and Caritas Internationalis reviewers; they must see a menu clearly better than the v2 build and a coherent component system they can approve for the Elementor site.
Constraints: Guide 2025 colours and type (pinned), caritas.org as quality bar, instant hover opening, real v2 content, sober motion, transposable to Elementor 4.
Build path: code-led (no image generation on this machine).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The menu is a map of the institution. One white panel stays open while the pointer travels; it says what each section is, lists its pages with a line each, and previews something real (a photo, the six zones, the latest declarations, the newest report). It refuses the plain dropdown list and the two-line wrapped bar of the current build.

OWN-WORLD: Caritas red #B11D23 used only for action and position (the sliding 3px indicator, buttons, links), black bold Helvetica (Heros self-hosted) for titles, white ground, sand #EBE3D7 / #F5F1EA bands, Baie #8B2113 footer with white logo, Georgia only for quotes. Square corners, 1px #DED8CE rules, photos full-bleed inside their frames with a small credit.

STORY: The reviewer hovers the menu and moves across sections without the panel closing; they scroll a calm guide (colours, type, buttons, cards, blocks, forms, photos, motion) and end on the burgundy footer, ready to approve.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Utility bar (membership, jobs, report abuse in red, contact, FR/EN/PT), main bar with logo left, five sections in one line, search and the red Faire un don button right. Below, the guide title "Menu et design global" with a short lead and a "Ouvrir le menu" prompt, a sticky contents rail on the left at desktop.

FORM: brief-pinned, no concept-seed roll; seed key: none. Authority for skipping the roll: the user's answer of 2026-10-28 ("Références: caritas.org", scope "Menu + guide de style", motion "Sobre, façon caritas.org"), the Caritas Internationalis brand guide 2025 pinned in PRODUCT.md, and the standing canon preference. Signature interaction: the red indicator slides between items while the single panel morphs its height and slides its content in the direction of travel.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
