## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS prototype in the shared `web_prototypes` catalog under `/caritas-v8/`. It is a design reference for the real site, which is WordPress + Elementor Pro 4 (atomic V4 widgets, Mega Menu widget, Theme Builder) on the dev install v2.caritas-africa.org. Everything shown must be transposable to Elementor: global classes, kit variables, kit custom CSS and a small mu-plugin script.

## Users

- **The client reviewers**: the Caritas Africa manager who asked for a better menu and global design (2026-10-28: "c'est basique, on peut avoir une meilleure version"), NM (Mme Nyrara), who asked to reproduce caritas.org's menu, and Caritas Internationalis.
- **The site's audiences** (confirmed for earlier versions): partners and funders, the 46 national Caritas and their staff, individual donors, candidates and service providers.

## Product Purpose

Caritas Africa is the Africa Region of Caritas Internationalis: the Catholic Church's social ministry in Africa, coordinating 46 national Caritas in 46 countries, with its Regional Executive Secretariat in Lomé, Togo. This mockup shows the site's header with a mega menu, its footer and a style sheet of the shared components, so the client can approve the global design before it is rolled out on the Elementor site.

Scope confirmed 2026-10-28: "Menu + guide de style" (header, mega menu, footer and a component sheet), not a full page. Motion: sober, caritas.org-like (sticky header, sliding panels, reveals, counters). Quality bar: caritas.org.

## Positioning

The continental coordination body of the Catholic Church's charity in Africa: it strengthens, coordinates and represents 46 national Caritas and speaks for them, rather than running field projects under its own name.

## Operating Context

## Brand Commitments

- Caritas Internationalis brand guide 2025 (v1.6): Rouge Caritas #B11D23 with lots of white; black #000; Baie #8B2113 for the footer; Sable #EBE3D7; secondary #60B4CD #E68922 #57B06D #CAC5B8, sparingly. Titles and text in Helvetica Neue (fallback Arial), bold or black, caps allowed, tight leading; Georgia (for Athelas) for quotes and stories. Colour logo on light grounds, white logo on dark, never altered; clear space = height of the plus; minimum 100 px wide.
- Standing preference: the category standard for an NGO site, played straight, at the craft level of the named reference (here caritas.org).
- Photos: authentic and dignified, always with words; credits small, consistent, at the bottom.

## Product Principles

- The menu is a map of the institution: every panel says what the section is before listing its pages.
- One system for the whole site, small enough to rebuild in Elementor.
- Real content only.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard and screen-reader access to the menu (WCAG 2.1 AA), visible focus, reduced-motion respected, readable on low-end Android phones (most visitors).
