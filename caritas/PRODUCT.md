# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS prototype (user's choice), deployed in the shared `web_prototypes` catalog under `/caritas/`. The live site is WordPress (OceanWP); a later transposition is possible but not in scope.

## Users

All four audiences were confirmed as priorities:

- **Partners and funders**: Caritas member organisations of the North and institutional donors (CAFOD, Misereor, Trócaire, Caritas Internationalis…). They need credibility, governance, annual reports, the strategic framework and programmes.
- **The Caritas network in Africa**: the 46 national Caritas organisations and their staff. They need resources, publications, the Knowledge HUB (DGroups KM4change) and network news.
- **Individual donors**: faithful, diaspora and the general public who want to give or get involved.
- **Candidates and service providers**: job offers, calls for tenders, terms of reference.

## Product Purpose

Caritas Africa is the Africa Region of the Caritas Internationalis confederation: the Catholic Church's social-pastoral ministry in Africa, coordinating 46 national Caritas organisations in 46 African countries (including the Atlantic and Indian Ocean islands). The Regional Executive Secretariat is in Lomé, Togo. The site must present who they are, give access to their publications and reports, publish jobs and tenders, collect donations, and let anyone report abuse (safeguarding).

## Positioning

The continental coordination body of the Catholic Church's charity in Africa: not a field NGO running projects under its own name, but the network that strengthens, coordinates and represents 46 national Caritas, and speaks for them on advocacy, humanitarian response and integral human development.

## Operating Context

- Trilingual publications (French, English, Portuguese); site language is French, with English content in places.
- Regional zones used by the network: ACEAC, ACERAC, AMECEA, CEDOI-M, IMBISA, RECOWA-CERAO.
- Safeguarding is a formal obligation (policy for the protection of children and vulnerable adults, anti-harassment policy, abuse reporting form).
- Donations currently run through GiveWP (broken receipt shortcode on the live site).

## Brand Commitments

- Keep the Caritas Africa logo (Caritas flame cross + "Caritas AFRICA" wordmark) and the Caritas red of the confederation. Everything else (typography, layout, secondary palette) is free.
- Standing preference (2026-09-30): the category standard for an NGO site, played straight, no novelty identity. Quality bar = the Caritas family (Caritas Internationalis, CAFOD, Trócaire) and MSF / ICRC (institutional credibility, documentary photography, visible donation, rigorous typography).

- There are no testimonials and no beneficiary counts for Caritas Africa itself. Do not invent impact figures.

## Product Principles

1. Network before brand: show the 46 Caritas and the zones; Caritas Africa exists to strengthen them.
2. Accountability is visible: reports, strategic framework, governance and safeguarding are one click away, never buried.
3. Faith stated plainly: scripture references and Church teaching are part of the identity, said with sobriety.
4. Every audience has a door: give, partner, find a resource, apply, report abuse.
5. Only verified facts and figures.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readers across Africa on mobile data and mid-range phones: light pages, WCAG AA, legible type. The content is multilingual (FR/EN/PT), so layouts must tolerate longer strings.
