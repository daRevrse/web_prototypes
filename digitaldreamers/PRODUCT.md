## Platform

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS prototype in the shared `web_prototypes` catalog under `/digitaldreamers/`. The live site (digitaldreamers.bostonsoluxacademy.com) is WordPress; a later transposition is possible but not in scope.

## Users

All confirmed as priorities:

- **Parents**: they register their child and need trust. They look for what the child will learn, who supervises, that it is free, the dates, the place and a contact.
- **Children aged 10–13**: they need to want to come. The hooks are games, AI, building a final project, and the atmosphere of the sessions.
- **Partners and companies**: potential sponsors and guest speakers, such as the IT director who came to meet the 2026 cohort.
- **Schools**: they relay the programme to their pupils.

## Product Purpose

Digital Dreamers is BostonSolux Academy's free digital-initiation programme for children aged 10 to 13, held in person during the July holidays at the BostonSolux Training Center in Kpogan (Togo). It exists to give children the skills of the future early. Success means registrations filled, parents confident, and children proud to present their project at the end.

## Positioning

A free, in-person, expert-led holiday programme run by an IT training and consulting firm, BostonSolux Academy. Children learn in the same training rooms as professionals, from trainers who work in the field. It has become a pathway over several years: Year 1 for beginners, Year 2 for advanced learners.

## Product Principles

1. Parents decide, children dream: every screen answers a parent's question and gives a child a reason to want to come.
2. Show the real room: real photos of real sessions, never stock images.
3. Free and simple: gratuity, dates, age and place are visible at once, and registration takes a minute.
4. Credibility through the academy: professional trainers, a professional training centre, certificates.
5. Only true facts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Parents mostly on phones and mobile data: light pages, large tap targets, WCAG AA. The registration form must be forgiving and clear.
