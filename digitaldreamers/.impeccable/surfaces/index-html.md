## Scope

Redesign of the Digital Dreamers prototype (digitaldreamers.bostonsoluxacademy.com): Accueil (index.html) and Pré-inscription (inscription.html). Mode: Persuade for the home, Operate for the pre-registration form.

## Audience and job

Parents decide and register; children aged 10–13 must want to come; partners and schools relay or take part. Action: pre-register a child for the next edition (dates to be confirmed), or call. Proof: real photos of the 2024, 2025 and 2026 sessions, real programme, real dates, real trainer quotes, free participation. The incumbent's invented facts (500+ pupils, 100 % online, Dakar/Abidjan events, fake news, fake offices) are removed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard for a youth coding programme, played straight at the craft of Code.org / Scratch and Code Club / Raspberry Pi Foundation: real children in real rooms, plain answers for parents, one obvious way to register. It refuses the incumbent's AI template (sparkle logo, eyebrow pills, radial glows, floating stat card, stock photos, hover-lifting cards, gradient bands).

OWN-WORLD: White ground, ink #1E1818, BostonSolux red #B40000 for brand, actions and highlights, a light red wash #FDF0EF and a warm grey band #F6F3F2, one ink band for the footer. Rubik 700 to 800 for display (chunky, friendly, not childish), Lexend for reading (designed for learners). Rounded but not bubbly: 14px photos, 18px panels, pill buttons. Authored line icons at 2px stroke in red discs.

STORY: A parent understands in one screen what Digital Dreamers is (free, 10–13 years, in person at Kpogan, code, AI, games, cybersecurity), sees that three editions already happened, trusts the academy, and pre-registers; a child sees kids like them and the games and projects they will make.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White header with the Digital Dreamers wordmark alone (the "par BostonSolux Academy" line was removed at the user's request), five anchors, red pill "Pré-inscrire mon enfant". Hero split 1fr / 1fr: left, "Offrez à votre enfant les compétences du futur." in Rubik 800 at clamp(2.3rem, 1.3rem + 2.8vw, 3.7rem), one sentence on the programme, four fact chips in a 2 by 2 grid (10 à 13 ans, gratuit, en présentiel à Kpogan, 3 éditions), red primary CTA and secondary "Voir le programme", one line on the pre-registration; right, the 2025 promotion group photo in their red and white polos, 18px radius, captioned. A red band under the hero thanks the 2026 promotion (20–31 July 2026).

SIGNATURE: the editions switcher (2026 / 2025 / 2024 tabs, each with its real photos, dates and highlights, active tab marked by a sliding red pill; amended after the finish review from an underline, because a filled segmented control reads as a control for children and parents on phones) and the pre-registration form with a live age check (date of birth → age in July of next year, the programme's usual month, stated as such) that explains itself instead of blocking silently. Motion grammar: ease-out cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1); press scale 0.97 at 160ms; tab pill slides 240ms; FAQ answers fade in; one load moment, the hero photo settling from scale 0.97 with opacity; reduced motion keeps opacity only.

FORM: canon (the youth-programme category standard, user-chosen standing exit), references Code.org / Scratch and Code Club / Raspberry Pi Foundation; seed key d648f687.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
