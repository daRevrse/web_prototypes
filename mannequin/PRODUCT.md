# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS prototype, deployed in the shared `web_prototypes` catalog under `/mannequin/` (catalog convention: one folder per mockup, relative paths, a catalog card in the root `index.html`). One long page for this first proposal (user's choice, 2026-10-26).

## Users

Primary (confirmed): professionals who book models. Model agencies (mother and placing agencies), fashion and beauty brands, photographers, stylists and casting directors, fashion-show and event organisers. They arrive from an Instagram bio link, a WhatsApp message or a comp card, and decide in seconds whether her look, measurements and experience fit a job, then ask about availability and rates.

Secondary: press and the public reading her story. They are served by the career section but are not who the page is built for.

## Product Purpose

A personal website for a fashion model and photographic model (mannequin et modèle photo) based in Dakar, Senegal. It shows her work, tells her career path (parcours), gives the facts a booker needs (measurements, agencies, experience) and turns interest into a booking request.

Success: a professional who lands on the page can judge her look, read her measurements and experience, and send a precise booking request without leaving the page.

## Positioning

Her own site, not an agency listing: one place where her book, her complete career path and her booking contact live together, under her name, in her market (Dakar, then West Africa and beyond).

## Operating Context

- Main market: Dakar. The fashion calendar there includes Dakar Fashion Week; any participation must be confirmed by the client before it appears as fact.
- The model industry's standard working objects: the book (portfolio), polaroids or digitals (unretouched natural photos that agencies always ask for), the comp card (carte composite: name, photos, measurements, agency) and the booking request (project type, dates, place, usage).
- Measurements are expressed in centimetres for this market (height, bust, waist, hips), plus shoe size (EU), hair and eye colour.
- Site language: French.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One long page: home, book, career path, comp card, booking contact.
- Booking contact through a form and WhatsApp; Instagram linked. The form is a mockup and sends nothing.
- No real content yet beyond what the user has kept for later: name, measurements and social links are provisional placeholders, clearly highlighted, to be replaced (same review bar pattern as `hope-logistics/`: yellow `.tbc` marks counted in a top bar with a toggle).
- No photos of the client yet: the book uses free stock model photographs (Unsplash), each labelled "photo provisoire" on the page.

## Brand Commitments

None yet. Her name is the identity; no logo, colours or fonts have been set.

## Evidence on Hand

- The user has her real name, measurements and social links but has not supplied them for this mockup; every one of them is a provisional placeholder on the page.
- No photographs of the client, no list of shows, campaigns, agencies, publications or clients, no press, no testimonials. Do not present any of these as fact: career entries, agencies, clients and press are provisional and highlighted, and stock photos are labelled.

## Product Principles

1. The photographs lead; everything else serves them.
2. A booker gets the facts in one look: measurements, base city, agency, contact.
3. The career path is told with real dates and named jobs, never vague claims.
4. Every provisional element is visibly provisional until the client confirms it.
5. Booking is always one tap away.
