# Design System: Khady Sène, carte composite

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Comp Card on the Seamless"**

The site is a printed working object photographed in a studio: one white matte card, with printer's crop marks at its corners, lying on a black seamless. Everything on the page is either that black ground or a piece of white stock placed on it: the card itself, the polaroids, the booking slip. The palette is achromatic so the photographs carry all the colour. Depth comes from paper on black, never from shadow.

Type is a single family, Sofia Sans, in three widths. The name and section titles are set in Extra Condensed capitals at poster scale, the measurements in Condensed tabular numerals, and everything else in the regular width in sentence case. The density is a booker's: facts in one look, hairline rules between lines, no ornament.

Motion is one grammar, the print developing, in two layers. The first layer develops the page: crop marks draw out, photos reveal from the bottom with a slow scale settle, display letters rise from their line, and the card turns over on scroll. The second layer handles the prints: an opening screen where the name rises and a 0 to 100 count runs before a black curtain lifts, the card tilting toward the pointer, measurements counting up, paragraphs rising line by line, polaroids that can be picked up and thrown, and a giant footer name rising letter by letter. All of it is gated on `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, and everything that follows the pointer is also gated on a fine, hovering pointer. Under reduced motion none of it runs: no opening screen, no smooth scroll, and the two card faces stack.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Black seamless (#000) alternating with near-white card stock; no other ground colours.
- One family in three widths; capitals only for the name, section titles, wordmark, ticker and booking band.
- Square corners everywhere (0px), 1px hairline rules, no shadows.
- Photos in plain rectangular windows with thin stock-coloured gutters.
- Print-native devices: crop marks, slug line under the card, polaroids, A5 print stylesheet.
- Motion is the print developing: things rise from a mask, reveal bottom-up, or are handled like loose prints.
- A yellow review layer marks provisional content and disappears once the client supplies facts.

## Colors

A strictly achromatic system: black, white stock and three greys, with yellow reserved for the provisional-content layer.

### Primary
- **Press Black** (ink): all type on stock, the primary button, the selected chip, the active filter underline, the parcours progress line, the reading-progress line under the header, the booking band on hover, and focus rings on light grounds.

### Neutral
- **Studio Seamless** (seamless): the ground of the opening screen, the card stage, the ticker, the polaroid band and the booking section. Same value as Press Black but a different role: it is the floor, not the ink.
- **Seamless White** (on-seamless): type and outlined ticker words on the seamless, the opening screen's name, count and loading bar, the pointer square (inverted by a difference blend, so it reads black on stock and white on the seamless), and focus rings on dark grounds.
- **Seamless Grey** (on-seamless-muted): secondary text on black, crop marks, the slug line, the opening screen caption, ticker dashes, contact labels and icons.
- **Matte Card Stock** (card-stock): the page ground of light sections, the booking band at rest, and every paper object placed on the seamless (card faces, polaroids, booking slip).
- **Graphite** (ink-secondary): descriptors, section intro text, career descriptions, footer text.
- **Pencil Grey** (ink-tertiary): measurement labels, units, fact labels, inactive filters, career years not yet reached, optional markers, notes.
- **Hairline** (hairline): every 1px rule on stock: header bottom, filter baseline, facts, career list, card back divider, booking band top, footer tops. On the seamless the equivalent rule is white at 16-22% opacity.
- **Field Stroke** (field-stroke): resting border of inputs and choice chips.
- **Proof Red** (error): validation messages and invalid field borders only.

### Provisional layer (mockup only)
- **Proof Yellow** (tbc-highlight), **Proof Ink** (tbc-ink), **Proof Rule** (tbc-line): the review marks and the review bar. They belong to the provisional-content layer described under Components and leave with it.

### Named Rules
**The Photographs Carry the Colour Rule.** No chromatic colour enters the system outside the photographs. Red is for errors, yellow for provisional marks; neither is decoration.

**The Two Grounds Rule.** A surface is either the black seamless or white stock. Light sections sit on stock; dark sections are seamless and anything placed on them is stock.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Sofia Sans Extra Condensed (with Arial Narrow)
**Numeral Font:** Sofia Sans Condensed (with Sofia Sans)
**Body Font:** Sofia Sans (with Segoe UI, system-ui)

All three are self-hosted variable woff2 files (weight axis 1 to 1000), so in-between weights (620, 650) are used deliberately.

**Character:** One family in three widths reads as a single printed voice: the narrowest width shouts the name like a poster, the condensed width tabulates measurements like a spec sheet, the regular width speaks plainly.

The ramp has three registers: poster steps in fluid viewport units, card steps in container units (they scale with the card, never with the window), and fixed functional steps for labels and captions.

### Hierarchy: poster steps (Extra Condensed, uppercase)
- **Footer Name** (620, clamp(4.5rem, 22vw, 20rem), 0.82, -0.01em): the name across the foot of the page.
- **Loader Name** (620, clamp(4.5rem, 16vw, 15rem), 0.84): the name on the opening screen; 40vw and one word per line under 640px.
- **Display** (620, clamp(3.75rem, 8vw, 6rem), 0.84): section titles (Book, Parcours, Booking).
- **Ticker** (620, clamp(3.25rem, 7.5vw, 6rem), 1): the services ticker.
- **Headline** (620, clamp(3.25rem, 6vw, 5rem), 0.84): the smaller display step (Polaroïds).
- **Loader Count** (620, clamp(3rem, 7vw, 6rem), 0.9, tabular): the 0-100 count on the opening screen.
- **Band** (620, clamp(2.5rem, 5.5vw, 4.5rem), 1): the "Demander un booking" band.
- **Wordmark** (650, 1.75rem, 0.015em): the name in header and footer.

### Hierarchy: card steps (container units)
- **Display Card** (620, 22cqh, 0.86, uppercase): the name on the card front, on two lines. In portrait (under 640px) it switches to one line at 23.5cqw.
- **Display Card Back** (620, 9.4cqh, 0.86, uppercase): the name on the card back, hidden in portrait.
- **Numeral** (600, clamp(20px, 5.6cqh, 46px), tabular lining figures): front measurements; clamp(18px, 3.8cqh, 26px) in portrait. Units drop to 0.42em (floor 11px) in Pencil Grey.
- **Numeral Back** (600, clamp(16px, 3.6cqh, 30px)): full stats on the back; 18px in portrait.
- **Card Role** (400, clamp(14px, 3cqh, 24px), 1.3): the descriptor under the name; clamp(13px, 2.6cqh, 15px) in portrait.
- **Card Agency** (400, clamp(13px, 2.5cqh, 19px)): the agency line; 13px in portrait.
- **Card Button** (600, clamp(14px, 2.4cqh, 18px)) and **Card Button Small** (600, clamp(13px, 2.1cqh, 16px)): buttons inside the card, at least 44px / 40px tall; 14px in the portrait front row.
- **Card Contact** (400, clamp(12px, 2.1cqh, 16px), 1.6): contact lines on the back.
- **Card Label** (400, clamp(11px, 2.2cqh, 16px), 1.2, Pencil Grey): measurement labels on the front; 11px in portrait.
- **Card Label Back** (400, clamp(11px, 1.8cqh, 14px), 1.2): measurement labels on the back.

### Hierarchy: text and functional steps (regular width, sentence case)
- **Lead** (400, clamp(1.125rem, 1.5vw, 1.3125rem), 1.5, max 34em): section introductions.
- **Title** (650, 1.125rem, 1.3): sub-headings inside a section.
- **Body List** (400, 1.125rem, 1.5, max 38em): career entries; 1.0625rem under 640px.
- **Body** (400, 1.0625rem, 1.55; 1rem under 640px): running text; also the mobile menu links.
- **Label** (600, 1rem, 1, 0.01em): buttons, filters (500) and polaroid captions (500, Condensed, 1.2).
- **Label Small** (500-600, 0.9375rem): nav links, the small header button, field labels, book captions, the polaroid hint, footer text, viewer caption, booking status, and the pointer tag ("Voir", "Lancer").
- **Caption** (0.875rem): field errors (500, Proof Red) and the booking note (Pencil Grey).
- **Caption Small** (0.8125rem): contact labels on the seamless; the filter count uses the same ratio as 0.8125em.
- **Meta** (500, 14px, 1.3): the opening screen caption; the review bar text and its button (700) use the same size.
- **Slug** (500, 13px/22px, 0.02em): the print slug under the card; 12px under 640px.
- **Proof Block** (600, 15px, 1.45) and **Proof Tag** (600, 12px): provisional layer only (missing-content boxes, "Photo provisoire" tags, zone labels). They leave with the layer.
- **Floor** (11px): the minimum any scaled text may reach (card labels in portrait, units, inline proof tags via max(11px, …)).

### Named Rules
**The Capitals Are the Name Rule.** Uppercase is reserved for the Extra Condensed display width: the name (card, loader, footer), section titles, wordmark, ticker and booking band. Every other line is sentence case.

**The Rank by Weight Rule.** Inside a list, rank comes from weight, not size: career entries use one size, with the year at 700 and the lead phrase at 650.

**The Card Scales as One Rule.** Text inside the card is sized in container units with a px floor and ceiling, so the card keeps its proportions as one printed object; nothing inside it is sized in rem or vw.

## Layout

A centred container of 1360px with a fluid gutter (clamp(16px, 4vw, 56px)). Light sections breathe at clamp(80px, 11vw, 150px) of vertical padding; the polaroid band at clamp(72px, 9vw, 120px). The header is a slim 60px sticky bar with a 2px reading-progress line on its bottom edge.

The opening screen is full-viewport seamless with the name and its caption/count row pinned to the bottom-left at the gutter, and the loading bar along the bottom edge. It runs once per session.

The first viewport is the card stage: a landscape 3:2 card sized to the remaining viewport height (width min(100%, 1240px, available height × 1.5)), centred on the seamless, with a print slug line under it. The front splits 44% photo / 56% text. Under 640px the card turns portrait (3:5) and the photo stacks above the text at 47%. All card-internal spacing and type are in container query units (cqh / cqw).

Two-column sections use an asymmetric 5fr / 7fr split (intro left, content right) with gaps of clamp(40px, 6vw, 96px); the parcours intro is sticky above 960px. Section heads pair the display title with a text column aligned to its baseline. The book grid is 4 columns (3 under 1100px, 2 under 640px) with 40px row and 14px column gaps; polaroids are 4 columns (2 under 960px). All multi-column layouts collapse to one column under 960px. The nav becomes a drop-down panel under 860px.

The page ends with a full-width booking band (stock, hairline top) and a footer whose last block is the giant name, separated by a hairline, with a "Haut de page" link at its top right.

Print is part of the layout: an A5 landscape page (210 × 148 mm) prints the card front then back and nothing else.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat. There are no drop shadows. Depth is the contrast of paper objects (card, polaroids, booking slip) lying on the black seamless, plus real 3D on the card: the scroll turn (perspective 2400px) and a slight tilt toward the pointer (perspective 1400px, at most 5deg / 4deg, with the portrait sliding the other way). A dragged polaroid rises above its neighbours by stacking order, not by shadow. The only box-shadow in the build is a 1px spread ring (`0 0 0 1px`) that thickens a field border on focus or error; it is a border, not elevation.

### Named Rules
**The Paper on Black Rule.** Lift is shown by placing white stock on the seamless, never by casting a shadow. Polaroids sit slightly rotated at rest (about -0.8deg / 0.6deg) and may land at up to ±9deg once thrown, because loose prints do.

## Shapes

Every corner is square (0px): buttons, fields, chips, the card, photo windows, the viewer controls, the pointer square, the parcours preview. Photos sit in plain rectangles: 3:4 for book shots, polaroids and the parcours preview, the card front portrait filling its column, four equal windows on the back. Rules are 1px hairlines; the active filter underline, the parcours progress line and the reading-progress line are 2px Press Black; the loading bar is 3px. Crop marks are 22px × 1px printer's ticks offset 12px from each card corner. Polaroids keep a 10px white border with a deeper bottom margin carrying a caption. Ticker and booking band words are separated by short 0.42em × 3px dashes.

## Components

### Buttons
Plain, typeset and square; the action reads by contrast, not decoration.
- **Shape:** square (0px), 48px tall (40px small), 1px border.
- **Ink:** Press Black on stock text, the primary action ("Demander un booking", "Envoyer la demande").
- **Line:** transparent with a 1px Press Black border; fills black on hover. Secondary actions on the card (flip, print).
- **Paper:** stock on the seamless, for actions placed on black.
- **Hover / Focus / Active:** colour transitions at 180ms; press scales to 0.97 with the out curve; hover only on fine pointers; 2px focus outline offset 3px, white on dark grounds.
- **Magnetic:** the primary actions (header button, card "Demander un booking", print, submit) follow the pointer by 30% horizontally and 40% vertically, press to 0.96, and spring back with an elastic ease on leave. Fine pointers with motion only; CSS then transitions colour only.
- Icons are 1.6-stroke inline SVG at 1.25em, leading the label.

### Chips (booking project type)
- **Style:** 42px tall, 16px side padding, 1px Field Stroke border, square.
- **State:** checked fills Press Black with stock text; hover darkens the border to black; invalid turns borders Proof Red.

### Filters (book categories)
- Text tabs on a hairline baseline, Pencil Grey at rest, count in tabular figures at 0.8125em. Active turns Press Black at 600 with a 2px underline that scales in from the left (220ms). Filtering re-lays the grid with a Flip transition.

### Cards / Containers
- **Comp card:** two faces of Matte Card Stock on the seamless, square, no border, no shadow; front photo plus name, descriptor, measurements, agency and actions; back four photos plus full stats and contact, divided by a hairline.
- **Booking slip:** a stock panel on the seamless with clamp(24px, 4vw, 48px) padding and a 22px internal rhythm; it settles in from a 2.5deg tilt, then its fields follow in order.
- **Polaroid:** stock frame, 10px border, condensed caption at 500.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white fill, 1px Field Stroke border, square, 48px tall, 10px × 14px padding; label above at 600; "facultatif" marker in Pencil Grey at 400.
- **Focus:** border and a 1px ring turn Press Black (160ms).
- **Error:** border and ring Proof Red, message below in Proof Red at 0.875rem/500, nudged in with a short elastic shake.

### Navigation
- Wordmark left, four links in Label Small, small ink button right. Without motion, hover draws a 1px underline left to right (220ms). With motion on a fine pointer, the underline is replaced by rolling letters: each link holds two stacked copies and the letters roll up one by one (0.45s, 18ms stagger). The "Haut de page" link uses the same roll. Under 860px the links move into a drop-down panel with hairline-separated rows and the CTA shortens to "Booking". With motion on, the header hides on scroll down and returns on scroll up.

A 14px white square with a difference blend, so it inverts what it covers (black on stock, white on the seamless). ## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep every surface either black seamless or white stock, and place paper objects on the seamless rather than inventing a third ground.
- **Do** set the name and section titles in Sofia Sans Extra Condensed capitals at 620, and everything else in sentence case.
- **Do** set measurements in Sofia Sans Condensed with tabular lining figures, units smaller and in Pencil Grey.
- **Do** keep corners square (0px) and separate content with 1px Hairline rules.
- **Do** size anything inside the card in container units with a px floor and ceiling so it scales as one printed object.
- **Do** pick text sizes from the documented ramp; functional text never drops below 11px.
- **Do** keep motion to the print-developing grammar (rising from a mask, bottom-up reveals, the card turn, prints handled like loose paper) and drop it entirely under reduced motion.
- **Do** gate every pointer-following effect (tilt, pointer square, magnetic buttons, rolling links, parcours preview, dragging) on a fine, hovering pointer.
- **Do** keep the card printable as an A5 front and back.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use drop shadows; depth is paper on black.
- **Don't** round corners on buttons, fields, photos or cards.
- **Don't** introduce chromatic accent colours; colour belongs to the photographs.
- **Don't** use Proof Yellow outside the provisional-content layer, and don't ship that layer once real content arrives.
- **Don't** set body or interface text in capitals.
- **Don't** add a second type family.
