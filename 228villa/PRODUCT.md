# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS prototype (user's choice), later transposable into the client's WordPress site (Houzez-style property theme today). No build step.

## Users

- **Diaspora & expatriates**: Togolese living abroad, expats and visitors booking from a distance a furnished villa for a short stay in Lomé. They cannot visit before paying, so they need to see the house and trust the agent.
- **Lomé residents**: families and professionals looking for a long-term rental.
- **Owners**: people who want 228 Villa to list and rent out their property ("Louer ma villa / Publier une annonce").
- **Companies / NGOs**: housing staff or missions; higher budgets, need reassurance and fast answers.

## Product Purpose

228 Villa is a Lomé-based rental agency/listing site for villas in Greater Lomé (Togo, country code +228, hence the name). Visitors browse villas, see photos, price, bedrooms/bathrooms, furnishing and utilities, then schedule a visit (in person or video call) or contact the agent (phone / WhatsApp / email). Owners can submit their property for rental. Success = qualified visit requests and WhatsApp contacts, plus owner submissions.

## Positioning

A small, human catalogue of vetted villas around Lomé with a named local agent who answers on WhatsApp and can do a video visit for people abroad. Not a mass classifieds portal.

## Operating Context

- Prices in XOF (FCFA) per month; each villa has a reference number (Villa-0001…).
- Visits schedulable at fixed slots (10:00–17:00), "En personne" or "Appel vidéo".
- Contact runs largely through WhatsApp; photos come from phones (WhatsApp exports), uneven quality.
- Neighbourhoods: Agoè, Agodeke / Kpogan (Route Nationale N°2), Kohé, Sagbado, Lomé.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Listing attributes on hand: title, address, reference, bedrooms, bathrooms, garage, year built, contract length (court/long), furnished, electricity, water, guarding, deposit (caution), description, photo gallery, video, map.
- Features on the current site: search/find a villa, gallery, compare (up to 4), favourites, schedule a visit, agent contact form, newsletter, news articles, owner submission form, login.
- Language: French.
- Undecided: online payment/booking (not present today; do not invent it).

## Brand Commitments

- Name "228 Villa" and the house pictogram logo are kept (user confirmed). Colours and typography are free to be redesigned.
- Standing preference (2026-09-30): the category standard for property rental, played straight, no novelty identity. Quality bar = Airbnb / Plum Guide (photo-first, clear listing, obvious booking, remote trust) and SeLoger / Rightmove (search and filters up front, dense comparable facts).

## Product Principles

1. The house is the proof: real photos and real facts (price, rooms, utilities) before any marketing line.
2. Reachable human: a named agent and WhatsApp are always one tap away.
3. Remote-first trust: someone in Paris or Montréal must be able to decide without visiting.
4. Honest numbers: price per month in FCFA, deposit, what is included, stated plainly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Many visitors on mid-range Android phones and variable connections: mobile-first, light pages, legible type, WCAG AA contrast.
